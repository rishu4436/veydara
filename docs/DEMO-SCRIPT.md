# Demo script (current build, ~90 s)

1. **Overview, disconnected** — "Start from what you own." Show the source-health drawer: Base scan and Aave oracle *Live verified*, CMC quotes keyless, CMC RWA *Unavailable* (no key yet).
2. **Watch the Aave V4 Equities Hub contract** (link under the watch form, or `/?watch=0xa4d5947Eb727A052bae69C593FfC84247EC9864E`). Real, block-pinned balances of USDC and eight Coinbase tokenized equities; CMC prices with provenance chips; exposure by underlying. (This is a protocol contract, not a personal wallet.)
3. **Open NVDAc → Collateral Passport.** Price triad: CMC issuer-token price vs CMC aggregate (unavailable without key) vs Aave V4 protocol oracle ("Coinbase NVDA"), with the disagreement flag. Explicit unknowns for rights/ratio/redemption/eligibility. Sources with retrieval dates.
4. **RWA Risk Clock** — market closed (weekend), next open in your local time, oracle age.
5. **Lending venues** — Aave V4 MAG7 listing with hub/spoke/feed and "borrowing not offered: caps, LTV, eligibility unverified".
6. **Proof Center** — the CMC credit ledger rows generated by steps 2–3 (expected vs reported credits, cache hits) and the capability matrix.

With a CMC key: step 3 shows the aggregate and other issuers' tokens (NVDAX, NVDAon…), and "Load 200 candles" draws the chart with EMA50 and S/R zones.
